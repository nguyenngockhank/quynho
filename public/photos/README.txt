PUT YOUR PHOTOS HERE
====================

Drop your family/anniversary photos in this folder, then reference them
in the data files inside /src/data/ by setting each `image` field, e.g.:

    image: '/photos/wedding.jpg'

Until you do, the site shows elegant gold placeholder cards automatically.

Recommended:
  • Portrait photos work best for the timeline & galaxy (e.g. 900×1100).
  • Keep files reasonably sized (< 500 KB each) for smooth 60fps loading.

Files used by:
  • /src/data/memories.ts  -> Page 2 galaxy
  • /src/data/book.ts      -> Page 3 memory book
